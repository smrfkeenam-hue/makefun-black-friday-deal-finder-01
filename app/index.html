How the prices were checked

Every price on this page was read on 27 November 2026, Black Friday, at the store's own listing — or in a deals source that names the store, which each card cites. The discount on a card is computed from the two prices shown, so the numbers always add up. Prices can change or sell out; check the store page before you buy.

NEW LOW means the lowest price ever seen for that item. STRONG PRICE means a deep cut on a genuinely good price. ROUTINE DEAL means a real discount, but a modest one. Where a store's own was-price differs from the list price, the card says so.

DealHunt is an independent price check, not a store. Store names and product names belong to their owners; the links go to the stores.
